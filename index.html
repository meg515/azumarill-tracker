Core collection rule

English + Japanese + Spanish are required for every card, plus any other language only when it's a genuinely unique regional release — different card number, artwork, product, stamp, or finish. Straight translations of a Core card are Global Extras, not required.

Completion

100% means all 257 Core rows are OWNED. ORDERED doesn't count until it arrives. SKIP never counts as completed.

Cameos & Bonus Oddities

Cards that aren't named Azurill, Marill, or Azumarill but feature one of them in the artwork — or documented error/manufacturing variants. Bonus only — they never affect Core completion.

Buying approach

For out-of-print cards, buy the single. Sealed product is for fun, not for chasing completion.
